About me

A lifetime with the violin

I started playing the violin at the age of five, and the instrument has been part of my routine and my identity ever since. Born in Belém, in the state of Pará, I am a violin student at the School of Music of the Federal University of Pará (UFPA), where I continue developing my technique and repertoire.

Today I put that experience to work for anyone looking for quality music for shows, events and ceremonies.
